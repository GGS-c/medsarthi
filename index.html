Structured 6-Stage Journey

Our Admission Process

A transparent, stress-free pathway from initial NEET assessment to final college enrollment.

Step 01

Initial Counselling

Personal discussion with the student and parents to understand aspirations, previous academic history, and financial preferences.

Step 02

Course Selection

Evaluating medical and allied disciplines (MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BHMS, BVSc, Physiotherapy) to choose the best clinical path.

Step 03

College Selection

Mapping student NEET scores against authentic previous cutoffs to formulate a realistic and strategic college preference list.

Step 04

Eligibility Guidance

Checking category requirements, domicile criteria, and state-specific counseling rules to prevent disqualification.

Step 05

Application & Docs

Hands-on support with portal registration, choice filling, document uploads, and tracking seat allotment rounds.

Step 06

Admission Support

Assisting with final physical reporting at the allocated college, fee submission compliance, and onboarding formalities.
