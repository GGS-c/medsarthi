Roadmap & Guidelines

Admission Process & Regulatory Roadmap

Understand every stage of the medical entrance counseling lifecycle: eligibility checks, document preparation, choice-filling strategy, and physical reporting.

Step-by-Step Execution

The 6-Step MedSarthi Admission Process

01

Step 1: Initial Counselling

Assessment

One-on-one consultation with Dr. Prasad Wani and senior mentors. We evaluate student aspirations, review NEET scorecards or expected percentiles, analyze previous academic history, and establish realistic options.

02

Step 2: Course Selection

Career Clarity

Evaluating career scopes between clinical medicine (MBBS), dental surgery (BDS), traditional AYUSH healthcare systems (BAMS, BHMS), veterinary science (BVSc), and physiotherapy (BPT) based on student inclination and eligibility.

03

Step 3: College Selection

Merit Mapping

Shortlisting accredited government and private colleges. We formulate a customized preference list mapping your state merit rank against previous year round-1 and round-2 closing cutoffs.

04

Step 4: Eligibility Guidance

Regulatory Audit

Verifying qualifying cutoffs, minimum 50% in 12th PCB (40% for reserved category), category reservation certificates, and state domicile requirements to safeguard eligibility.

05

Step 5: Application & Documentation

Execution

Live assistance with online portal registration, accurate choice locking, document scans verification, and tracking allotment results across rounds.

06

Step 6: Admission Support

Enrollment

Guiding students through nodal centre verification, bond affidavits, tuition fee remittance procedures, and physical reporting at the allocated college campus.

Checklist for Candidates

Required Documents for Admission Counselling

Prepare original sets and multiple photocopies prior to counseling registration:

NEET-UG Admit Card & Scorecard
10th Standard Marksheet & Passing Certificate
12th Standard Marksheet (PCB Subjects)
State Domicile Certificate (MP / Maharashtra)
Caste Certificate & Caste Validity (If Applicable)
Non-Creamy Layer Certificate (OBC/EWS)
Government Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar / Passport)
Passport Size Photographs (Matching NEET Admit Card)
Medical Fitness Certificate & Transfer Certificate (TC)
