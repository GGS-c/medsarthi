The MedSarthi Advantage

Our Stated Counselling Services

Transparent, client-verified advisory services designed to eliminate confusion at every stage.

Service 01

MBBS Admission Guidance

Complete assistance with registration on state counseling portals (such as DME Madhya Pradesh, CET Cell Maharashtra) and All India Quota (MCC). We guide you through choice filling sequences based on merit and category reservation.

Service 02

NEET Eligibility Guidance

Detailed evaluation of your NEET scorecard against official category percentiles (50th percentile for General/EWS, 40th for SC/ST/OBC), 12th PCB percentage verification, and domicile validation.

Service 03

Application & Documentation

Avoid technical rejections with our thorough verification checklist. We review caste validity, non-creamy layer, domicile certificates, gap affidavits, and bond documentation required at nodal reporting centers.

Service 04

Course Counselling

Objective, realistic comparison between clinical allopathy (MBBS), dental surgery (BDS), traditional AYUSH healthcare (BAMS & BHMS), veterinary practice, and physiotherapy so students choose their true calling.

Service 05

College Selection Guidance

Strategically aligning your NEET score with previous year cutoffs, fee structures, clinical bed capacities, and teaching faculty quality to formulate an optimized preference order.

Services 06 & 07

Admission Support & 1-on-1 Mentorship

Round-by-round guidance through allotment updates, upgradation choices, physical reporting at allocated colleges, and direct access to Dr. Prasad Wani for personalized doubt resolution.
